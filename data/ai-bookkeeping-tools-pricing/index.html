Which tools are a ledger, and which sit on top of one?

The price only compares fairly once you know what else you have to pay for. ZeraBooks is its own general ledger. So is Docyt, and Digits keeps an automated ledger of its own. Booke AI works inside QuickBooks Online or Xero and says it is not a new ledger, so its $129 comes on top of that plan. Dext, Hubdoc, AutoEntry and Nanonets capture documents and hand the data to a ledger or a spreadsheet; none of them keeps the books. Docyt also syncs to QuickBooks Online for customers who want to keep it for statutory reporting.

What doesn't ZeraBooks do?

Does ZeraBooks do this?Answer
Bank feedsNo. ZeraBooks works from the statements you upload and proves each one against its printed opening and closing balances.
QuickBooks or Xero syncNo. ZeraBooks is its own general ledger. You move your books over once with a trial balance and aging reports.
.qbo or IIF filesNo. The QuickBooks-ready download is a CSV with Date, Description and Amount for QuickBooks Online's bank upload.
Property management or point-of-sale integrationsNo. Hotel and restaurant books are kept from statements, bills and invoices.
Bill payNo. Bills are recorded and matched to the bank payment, not paid from ZeraBooks.
Several currencies in one companyNo. Each company keeps its books in one currency: US dollars, Canadian dollars or euros.

FAQ

How much does Booke AI cost?

Booke AI lists its AI Bookkeeper at $129 per business a month, and it works inside QuickBooks Online or Xero, so that plan is paid on top. Pricing for accounting firms is by request. Checked November 6, 2026.

How much does Docyt cost?

Docyt doesn't publish prices. Its pricing page says it charges per property per month on annual plans, depending on the size and type of each property and the modules chosen, with a quote usually within one business day. Checked November 6, 2026.

How much does Digits cost?

Digits listed Essentials at $65 and Core at $100 a month, with Pro at $250 a month coming soon, and a 30-day free trial. Checked October 1, 2026.

How much does ZeraBooks cost?

ZeraBooks is $79 a month billed annually ($948 a year) or $149 monthly, with the general ledger and AI bookkeeping, plus 1,000 converter pages a month, after a 7-day trial. Accounting firms get 3 client companies included and pay $29 a month per extra client.

Does ZeraBooks have bank feeds?

No. ZeraBooks works from the bank and card statements you upload and proves each one against its printed opening and closing balances, so a missing or doubled line shows up before a month closes.

Is the ZeraBooks bookkeeping service included in the software price?
