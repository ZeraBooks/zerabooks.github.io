README

Month-end checklists

A month-end close checklist lists, in order, the steps that make a month's books final: collect the statements, reconcile every bank and card account to its printed closing balance, post adjustments, review the reports and lock the month. Each folder here holds that checklist in Markdown and a CSV tracker with one row per step.

Folder For Guide
small-business/ A small business with a few bank and card accounts, closing its own books or with a bookkeeper Month-end close checklist, step by step
restaurant/ A restaurant: POS sales tied to card, cash and delivery app deposits, tips and sales tax held for others, inventory counts Bookkeeping for restaurants

How to use these files

  • Copy checklist.md into a GitHub issue or task tool at the start of each month and tick the boxes as you go.
  • Save a copy of the CSV tracker per month, put real names in Owner and mark each step done as it finishes.
  • Work the steps in order. If a reconciliation won't tie, stop and fix it before posting anything later in the list.
