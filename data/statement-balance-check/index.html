README

Statement balance check

A converted bank statement is complete only when the opening balance plus every transaction equals the closing balance the bank printed, to the cent. check_balance.py checks that, walks the running balance row by row, and tells you the first row where it breaks and whether a sign is flipped or a line is missing.

A bank statement converter turns a PDF statement into rows of date, description and amount. Most of the ways that goes wrong (a dropped line, a deposit read as a withdrawal, one misread digit) leave the totals off by a specific amount. This script finds that amount and points at the row.

Files

File What it is
check_balance.py The checker. Python 3, standard library only. Reads Amount (money out negative) or Money In and Money Out columns, plus an optional Balance column.
sample-ties.csv A fictional September 2026 business checking statement: opening 4,210.55, five rows, closing 5,622.13. It ties.
sample-sign-flip.csv The same month with the payroll payment read as money in.
sample-missing-line.csv The same month with the payroll payment left out.

All figures are fictional.

How to use it

  1. Convert the statement to CSV with a header row: Date,Description,Amount and, if the statement prints one, Balance. Separate Money In and Money Out columns work too.

  2. Run it with the opening and closing balances printed on the statement:

    python3 check_balance.py sample-ties.csv --opening 4210.55 --closing 5622.13

  3. If there is a Balance column you can leave out --opening; the script works it out from the first printed balance.

  4. Exit code 0 means it ties. Exit code 1 means it doesn't, so the script can sit in a batch job and stop bad files before they are imported.

What the output tells you

  Printed balance 3,842.13, worked out 8,078.97, off by 4,236.84.

On sample-missing-line.csv:

  Printed balance 5,654.13, worked out 7,772.55, off by 2,118.42.
  Likely cause: a line of -2,118.42 is missing between 09/05/2026 and 09/19/2026, ...
Gap against the printed balance Usual cause
Exactly twice one row's amount That row's sign is flipped, money in read as money out or the other way round
The amount of a real transaction That line was dropped, often at a page break or a section heading
A small amount that divides by 9 Two digits swapped on one row, such as 1,650.00 read as 1,560.00
Any other amount One amount misread by that much; compare the rows since the last good balance with the PDF

The check by hand

For the tied sample: 4,210.55 + 3,400.00 - 1,650.00 - 2,118.42 + 1,812.00 - 32.00 = 5,622.13. In a spreadsheet, put the opening balance in a cell and compare opening + SUM(Amount) with the printed closing balance. A result of 0.00 means every row arrived.

FAQ

Why check the running balance and not just the closing balance?

The closing balance tells you something is wrong. The running balance tells you where. Two errors can also cancel out at the end of the month and still show up in the middle.

What about credit card statements?

The same idea with different words: previous balance plus charges minus payments and credits equals the new balance. Put charges as negative amounts and payments as positive, and the script works on the card's balance owed.

My statement prints a balance only on some rows. Does that work?

Yes. Leave the Balance cell blank where the statement leaves it blank. The script checks at each row that has one.
