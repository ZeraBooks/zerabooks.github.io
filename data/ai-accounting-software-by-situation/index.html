Is AI accounting software worth it for a small business?

It is when the AI does the monthly work, not just the data entry. The test is whether the month ends reconciled to the bank without you. ZeraBooks closes each month from your statements and checks it against the printed balances; most other tools still leave categorizing and reconciling to you. What should a startup look for?

Closed months and reports an investor can read: a P&L, balance sheet and cash flow that tie to the bank. Startups also fall behind, so catch-up matters as much as the monthly routine. See the startup accounting software ranking for how ZeraBooks, Puzzle, Digits and Zeni handle that.

What is different for freelancers and the self-employed?

Invoicing and getting paid come first, then a year that is categorized when your accountant asks. ZeraBooks sends invoices and chases late ones. FreshBooks is strong on invoicing too. More in accounting software for the self-employed.

What if I'm switching from Digits, Puzzle or Wave?

Each switch has a different reason. Digits serves U.S. entities only; Puzzle drafts the books for you to approve and meters its AI in credits; Wave's free plan leaves the work to you.
