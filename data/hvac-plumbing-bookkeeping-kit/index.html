README

HVAC and plumbing bookkeeping kit

Bookkeeping for an HVAC or plumbing company separates service calls from installs, holds maintenance agreements and install deposits as money owed until the work is done, books financed jobs at full price with the dealer fee as a cost, and tracks parts on the trucks. This kit has the accounts, a checklist, a plan schedule and a worked month.

Files

File What it is
chart-of-accounts.csv A starting chart of accounts for a residential service company: service, install and plan revenue apart, truck stock as an asset, customer deposits and deferred plan revenue as liabilities. Columns: Number, Name, Type, Detail, Notes.
month-end-checklist.md Nine steps for closing the month on accrual books.
maintenance-plan-schedule.csv Thirty fictional 240.00 yearly plans sold in October 2026, earned 600.00 a month over twelve months, with the deferred balance at each month end.
financed-jobs.csv A fictional financed install: job price, dealer fee rate, dealer fee and the payout from the lender.
october-2026-month.csv The fictional company's October 2026 on accrual books: every line, its account, and whether it counts in profit.
check_trade_month.py Checks the financed jobs and the plan schedule, ties them to the month, and prints profit plus the money that reached the bank but is not earned yet. Python 3, standard library only.

How to use it

  1. Import chart-of-accounts.csv into your accounting software, or compare it with the accounts you have. Commercial contractors add job accounts and retainage.

  2. Keep a plan schedule like maintenance-plan-schedule.csv for each month's plan sales, and a row in financed-jobs.csv for each financed job.

  3. Run the check:

    python3 check_trade_month.py financed-jobs.csv maintenance-plan-schedule.csv october-2026-month.csv

  4. Work through month-end-checklist.md before you lock the month.

Employer payroll taxes 1,292.85
Van fuel and leases 3,140.00
Expenses 40,742.85
Profit 13,157.15

Two amounts reached the bank but are not in profit: a 6,000.00 deposit for a November furnace install (customer deposits) and 6,600.00 of the 7,200.00 in plan sales, which will be earned over the next eleven months (deferred maintenance revenue). Counted as sales, they would have shown October 12,600.00 better than it was.

FAQ

Cash or accrual books for a service company?

Many small shops start on cash basis. Once you sell maintenance agreements or take install deposits, accrual books show the real month, because they hold that money until it is earned. Your accountant decides what the tax return uses.

How do I record a financed job?

Book the full job price as revenue and the dealer fee as a financing fee. The lender pays you the job less the fee, so booking only the payout makes every financed job look cheaper than the price you quoted.

Are parts on the trucks an expense?

Not until they go into a job. Parts bought and still on a van are truck stock, an asset. Booking every supply house invoice straight to job cost makes some months look terrible and others too good.
