README

CAS monthly close templates

CPA firms automate client accounting services by letting software run each client's monthly cycle: statements in, transactions categorized from that client's history, every account reconciled to its printed balance and the month closed with reports. Staff answer questions, review what doesn't tie and sign off. These templates track that cycle across every client.

Files

File What it is
cas-monthly-cycle.csv The six steps of a CAS client's month: what software can do, what staff do, the evidence to keep and when the step counts as done.
cas-service-menu.csv A menu of CAS services (bookkeeping, reconciliations, monthly close, reporting, bill pay, payroll entries, year-end handoff) with what the client sends and gets. Use it to write an engagement scope.
client-close-tracker.csv One row per client per month, one column per step, with preparer and reviewer. Filled in with six fictional clients.
close_status.py Reads the tracker and prints each client's next open step. It flags a month signed off before every account tied or entries were approved, and a preparer reviewing their own work. Python 3, standard library only.
review-controls-checklist.md The controls to check before letting software do a client's month, and the per-client monthly checks.

How to use it

  1. Agree the scope with each client from cas-service-menu.csv. Delete the services you don't offer.

  2. At the start of each month, copy client-close-tracker.csv, set the Month column and change every step to No.

  3. Mark each step Yes as it's done. Put the reason in Notes when a client is stuck.

  4. Check the board:

    python3 close_status.py client-close-tracker.csv

    It exits with code 1 if it finds a control problem, so you can run it in a scheduled job and get alerted.

FAQ

What is client accounting services (CAS)?

CAS is the outsourced accounting a CPA firm does for clients during the year: bookkeeping, reconciliations, the monthly close, reporting and often bill pay and payroll. It is billed monthly, which makes the repeated monthly work the part worth automating.

Is the AI doing the accounting?

It should only read documents, suggest categories and draft entries. The ledger calculates the numbers, staff approve what is proposed, and judgment and sign-off stay with the firm.

Why track preparer and reviewer separately?

A second person catches what the first missed. A tracker that shows the same initials in both columns is a review that didn't happen.
