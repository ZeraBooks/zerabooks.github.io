README

Veterinary practice bookkeeping kit

Bookkeeping for a veterinary practice keeps exam and service revenue apart from pharmacy and food sales, books drug and supply costs apart from outside lab fees, grosses card and patient financing deposits back up before their fees, and splits each equipment loan payment into interest and principal. This kit has the chart of accounts, a checklist and a worked month.

Files

File What it is
chart-of-accounts.csv A starting chart of accounts for a general practice: services and pharmacy as separate income, drugs, labs and cremation as separate costs, client deposits and equipment loans. Columns: Number, Name, Type, Detail, Notes.
month-end-checklist.md Nine steps for closing a clinic's month, from grossing up deposits to locking the period.
gross-ups.csv A fictional card batch and patient financing payout, each grossed back up to the full bill with the fee on its own column.
loan-payments.csv A fictional equipment loan payment split into interest and principal.
september-2026-month.csv The same fictional clinic's September 2026 on cash basis: every line, its account, and whether it counts in profit.
check_vet_month.py Checks the gross-ups and the loan split and prints revenue, expenses and profit for the month. Python 3, standard library only.

How to use it

  1. Import chart-of-accounts.csv into your accounting software, or compare it with the accounts you have. Specialty and emergency hospitals usually split service revenue further.

  2. Each month, fill in copies of gross-ups.csv from the processor statement and financing payout reports, and loan-payments.csv from the lender statements.

  3. Run the check:

    python3 check_vet_month.py gross-ups.csv loan-payments.csv september-2026-month.csv

  4. Work through month-end-checklist.md before you lock the month.

FAQ

Is a vet clinic's bookkeeping the same as a human medical clinic's?

Partly. Payroll and equipment loans work the same. The difference is who pays: most pet insurance reimburses the pet owner after the visit, so the clinic is paid in full at checkout and has few insurance deposits to chase. The work is in the card and cash deposits and in running a pharmacy.

How do I record a patient financing deposit?

Book the full bill as service revenue and the provider's fee as an expense. The deposit is the bill less the fee, so booking only the deposit makes every financed visit look cheaper than it was.

Is the whole equipment loan payment an expense?

No. Only the interest is. The principal pays the loan down, so it goes against the equipment loan balance, and the equipment itself is depreciated.
