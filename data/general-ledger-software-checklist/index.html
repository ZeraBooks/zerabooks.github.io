General ledger software checklist

General ledger software is the system of record for a business's books: it holds every account and transaction and produces the financial statements. To choose one, check that it is true double-entry, refuses entries that don't balance, locks closed months, logs every change, and builds every report from the same ledger, then test it with the same entries.

This repo has the checklist, a requirements sheet to score candidates side by side, a migration checklist, and a fictional test month you can enter in any ledger to see whether its reports come out right.

Files

chart-of-accounts.csv An 11-account chart for the fictional test business.
test-journal.csv Eight balanced October 2026 entries: owner money in, equipment on the card, prepaid insurance, an invoice, rent, a customer payment, one month of insurance and one month of depreciation.
test-journal-unbalanced.csv The same batch with entry 5 off by 100.00. A good ledger posts none of it.
test-closed-period.csv One entry dated October 31. After October is closed, a good ledger blocks it.
check_journal.py Checks a journal batch (every entry balances, accounts exist, nothing in a closed period) and prints the trial balance, profit and loss and balance sheet totals it should produce. Python 3, standard library only.

All figures are fictional.

How to use it

  1. Copy requirements.csv and put the names of the products you are looking at in place of Candidate A and Candidate B.

  2. In a trial of each one, import chart-of-accounts.csv and test-journal.csv, then compare its reports with the expected results below.

  3. Try test-journal-unbalanced.csv and test-closed-period.csv and note what each product does.

  4. Check any batch of your own before you import it:

    python3 check_journal.py test-journal.csv chart-of-accounts.csv
python3 check_journal.py test-closed-period.csv chart-of-accounts.csv --closed-through 2026-10-31

FAQ

What is the best general ledger software?

It depends on who keeps the books. Large companies run ERP ledgers such as NetSuite or Sage Intacct; small businesses often use QuickBooks Online or Xero. Score the ones you are considering on requirements.csv with the same test month, and the differences show up quickly.

What is an AI general ledger?

A ledger where AI does routine bookkeeping inside the system of record: reading statements, categorizing transactions and proposing entries. The ledger should still do every calculation, and every AI change should be logged with a way to undo it. The Automation rows in requirements.csv test for that.

Do I have to bring every old transaction into the new ledger?

No. Start the new ledger from the old trial balance at a handoff date, plus open invoices, open bills and uncleared bank items. Keep the old books for the history.
