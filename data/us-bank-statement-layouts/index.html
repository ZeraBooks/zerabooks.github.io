U.S. bank statement layouts: Citi, U.S. Bank, TD Bank, PNC and Amex

Citi, U.S. Bank, TD Bank, PNC and American Express each print their statements differently, and most conversion errors come from those differences, not from misread digits. This dataset lists what each bank typically prints, what breaks when the PDF is turned into rows, and the check that catches it. ZeraBooks uses the same checks to prove every converted statement against its printed balances.

Files: layouts.csv (20 rows: bank, statement part, how it typically prints, what breaks, the check) and sample-months.csv (five fictional months, one per bank, every row with the balance the statement would print).

How does ZeraBooks prove a converted statement?

ZeraBooks Convert reads each statement page as an image, keeps every amount as printed (trailing minus, CR, DR, parentheses), takes the year from the statement period, leaves out printed balance lines and section totals, and drops a recap table (such as a checks paid summary) only when the account still ties without it. Then it proves each account, and each card member on a card statement, against the opening and closing balances the bank printed. FAQ

Why does a PNC statement start with a balance like 612.40-?

PNC typically prints a negative balance with the minus after the number. 612.40- means the account opened the period overdrawn by $612.40. A converter that drops the trailing minus reads the opening as positive and the whole month comes out wrong by twice the overdraft, $1,224.80 in the sample.

Why do U.S. Bank statement dates have no year?

U.S. Bank typically prints transaction dates like Jan 6 and states the year only in the statement period, which rarely matches a calendar month. On a Dec 22 2025 through Jan 21 2026 statement, December rows belong to 2025. Take the year from the period, not from today's date.

Is a Citi relationship statement one statement or several?

Several. Citi can combine checking, savings and CDs onto one monthly PDF, and each product has its own opening and closing balance. Convert and prove each product on its own; a transfer between them shows on both.

What does the asterisk in TD Bank's Checks Paid list mean?

TD Bank typically marks a break in the check number sequence with an asterisk. Check 2043* after 2041 means 2042 hasn't cleared in this period. It is not a missing row if the statement still ties.

How do I prove an Amex statement converted correctly?

Previous Balance minus Payments/Credits plus New Charges, Fees and Interest must equal the New Balance, and each card member's charges must add to that member's printed total. Travel detail and foreign spend lines carry no dollar amount of their own.
