README

Trucking bookkeeping checklist

A monthly trucking bookkeeping checklist: collect every settlement, fuel card and bank statement; book settlements gross; reconcile each bank, card and fuel card account to its statement; split truck loan payments; check escrow and factoring reserve balances; total fuel by state for IFTA; then review cost per mile against last month.

This repository holds that list in Markdown, a CSV tracker with one row per step, and the quarterly and yearly filing dates the monthly work feeds.

Files

File What it is
checklist.md The collect list, the nine monthly steps in order, the quarter and year items, and the five things trucking books most often miss. Tick boxes throughout.
close-tracker.csv 19 rows with Owner, Due day, Done and Notes columns. Due day counts working days after month end and is a starting suggestion; change it to suit.
filing-calendar.csv IFTA, U.S. estimated tax, Form 2290, IRP and the income tax return, with the period, due date, who usually files and what the books supply.

How to use it

  1. At the start of each month, copy checklist.md into a GitHub issue or your task tool, or print it.
  2. Save a copy of close-tracker.csv per month, for example close-2026-10.csv, put real names in Owner and mark Done as you go.
  3. Don't reconcile the bank before the settlements are booked. Each step uses the one before it, so doing them out of order means doing them twice.
  4. In the last month of each quarter, check filing-calendar.csv and have the fuel by state and year-to-date profit ready before the due date.

The general close that every business does is in month-end-checklists. This list only carries what is specific to trucking. A trucking chart of accounts is in chart-of-accounts-templates/trucking.

FAQ

How long does a trucking month-end take?

For one truck with one carrier, an hour or two once the documents are together. Waiting for fuel card and loan statements usually takes longer than the work. Each extra truck, card or factoring account adds time.

Do I need to reconcile the fuel card?

Yes. A fuel card is a credit account with its own statement and balance. Reconciling it is how you catch fills booked twice or missing, and it is where your IFTA fuel figures come from.

What if a settlement is missing?

Ask the carrier for it before closing the month. Booking only the deposit loses the gross revenue and every deduction for that week, and the escrow balance stops matching.
