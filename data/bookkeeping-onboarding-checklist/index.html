README

Bookkeeping onboarding checklist

To start with a new bookkeeper, a business needs to agree the last month it trusts, list every bank, card and loan account, and send statements for every month since, plus the old trial balance, unpaid invoice and bill lists and any payroll and sales tax reports. These files make that list and find the gaps.

Files

File What it is
onboarding-checklist.md The decisions, account list and documents to settle before the first month, with U.S. and Canada items.
document-request.csv 14 documents a bookkeeper asks for, why each is needed, the period, who it applies to and a Received column.
accounts-inventory.csv Every account the business has had and the months a statement is needed for. Fictional sample rows.
statements-received.csv One row per statement received, by account and month. Fictional sample rows.
check_statement_coverage.py Compares the two and lists every missing month per account, plus statements for accounts not on the inventory. Python 3, standard library only.
first-month.md What happens week by week in the first month of a remote bookkeeping engagement.

How to use it

  1. On the first call, agree the last month you trust and fill in accounts-inventory.csv with every account and the months needed.

  2. Send the business owner document-request.csv. Delete rows that don't apply.

  3. As statements arrive, add a row to statements-received.csv.

  4. Find the gaps:

    python3 check_statement_coverage.py accounts-inventory.csv statements-received.csv

  5. Ask for what's missing. Start the bookkeeping once the script says 0 statements missing.

The sample

The fictional business trusts its books through June 2026 and needs July to October. The script finds savings missing August and September, the loan missing July and August, and a statement for an "Old payroll account" that nobody listed. That last one matters most: an account the owner forgot is the usual reason a first month won't tie. All figures are fictional.

FAQ

How far back do the statements need to go?

To the last month the books can be trusted. If the old books were never reconciled, that may be the start of the year or earlier, and the work is a cleanup before ongoing bookkeeping.

Why statements and not a bank export?

A statement prints the opening and closing balance for the month, which is what each month is reconciled to. An export or app screen usually doesn't.

Do I need to sort the documents before sending them?

No. Send everything you have. Sorting is part of the bookkeeper's job, and the coverage check finds what's still missing.
