Accounting and bookkeeping statistics 2026

U.S. accountants and auditors earned a median $83,680 a year in May 2025 and held about 1.6 million jobs, projected to grow 5% by 2035. Bookkeeping clerks earned a median $50,670 and their jobs are projected to fall 6%. This repository has 51 figures like these in one CSV, each linked to its BLS, Census, SBA or IRS source.

How to use it

  1. Open data/statistics.csv in Excel or Google Sheets, or load it with any CSV reader. Filter on the id prefix to get one group: pay, outlook, firms, clients or counts.
  2. Read the unit column before you quote a number. Receipts for NAICS 5412 are in thousands of dollars, as Census publishes them; some other dollar figures are in billions.
  3. Quote the original source and period next to any figure, then cite this collection.

Why the counts of accountants differ

Three BLS programs count accountants and auditors three ways, and none is wrong:

Source Accountants and auditors What it counts
Occupational Outlook Handbook (2025) 1,595,200 All jobs, including self-employed
OEWS (May 2025) 1,449,500 Wage-and-salary jobs reported by employers
Current Population Survey (2025) 1,766,000 People who say this is their job, from a household survey

Wages match between the Handbook and OEWS because the Handbook uses OEWS wages. The CPS 2025 averages leave out October.

Notes on the data

  • Every figure was checked against its primary source on November 4, 2026.
  • Percentages noted as "our math" are computed from the cited table and say so in the notes column.
  • None of these are ZeraBooks figures.
  • BLS publishes new wage data each spring and new projections each fall, so check the source page before quoting a figure next year.
