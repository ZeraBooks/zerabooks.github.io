Credit card statement proof: Discover, American Express, Capital One and Citi

A card statement is proven the other way round from a bank statement: the previous balance plus new charges (fees and interest included), minus payments and credits, must equal the new balance. This dataset gives that formula as Discover, American Express, Capital One and Citi print it, with four fictional statements that tie and the printed lines that aren't transactions. ZeraBooks runs this check on every card statement it converts.

Files: issuers.csv (4 rows: issuer, summary it prints, the proof, printed lines that aren't transactions, where the PDF is) and sample-statements.csv (four fictional card statements, with card member where printed).

Why is a card statement proven differently from a bank statement?

On a bank statement, deposits raise the balance and withdrawals lower it. On a card, the balance is what you owe, so charges raise it and payments lower it. ZeraBooks keeps the books view in the file: a charge is money out, so it is negative, and a payment is positive. Both views have to agree. In every sample below, the new balance comes out of the issuer's formula, and the file total equals the previous balance minus the new balance.

What do the sample statements look like?

Each sample is a fictional business. In the converted file, charges are negative and payments and credits positive, so the Amount column adds up to how much the balance went down. That is the second check on every sample. Rows are in sample-statements.csv.

Discover: Ridgeback Pottery, 08/27/2026 to 09/26/2026

Paid part of the last balance, so interest was charged. Payments 1,500.00, credits 36.25 (Cashback Bonus redeemed as a statement credit), purchases 1,499.12, interest 21.94. The proof: 2,413.08 − 1,500.00 − 36.25 + 1,499.12 + 21.94 = 2,397.89. The file adds to 15.19, which is 2,413.08 − 2,397.89. The Cashback Bonus earned this period moved no money and isn't in the file. Two card members. Ana Brooks: charges 1,064.40, less a 120.00 return, 944.40. Theo Grant: charges 1,370.96. New Charges 2,435.36. The proof: 3,105.62 − 3,105.62 − 120.00 + 2,435.36 = 2,315.36. Capital One: Copperline Electric LLC, October 2026

A 5,000.00 payment left part of the balance unpaid, so 46.71 interest was charged on top of 4,539.78 in charges. The proof: 5,602.33 − 5,000.00 + 4,539.78 + 46.71 = 5,188.82. How does ZeraBooks prove a card with several card members?

When a card statement prints a total for each card member, ZeraBooks Convert assigns every charge to its card member and checks each member's rows against that printed total, as well as the statement as a whole. In the Amex sample, Ana Brooks has 1,064.40 in charges and a 120.00 return, so her rows net to 944.40, and Theo Grant's rows add to 1,370.96. The download can be split one file per card member. FAQ

What is the formula to check a credit card statement?

Previous balance, minus payments and credits, plus purchases, cash advances, balance transfers, fees and interest, equals the new balance. Discover and American Express print each of these on the account summary.

Is Cashback Bonus a transaction on a Discover statement?

Only when it is redeemed as a statement credit, which lowers what you owe and is listed with payments and credits. Cashback Bonus earned or carried over moved no money and is left out.

Do Amex foreign spend lines count as charges?

No. The foreign spend line under a charge shows the amount in the other currency. The dollar charge above it is the transaction.

Should I convert the credit card block on my Citi banking statement?

No. Citi says that block is summary information as of your last credit card statement. Convert the card statement itself.

Where are my Discover statements now?

Discover became part of Capital One on May 18, 2025. Capital One says card accounts move to its website and app in 2026 and early 2027. Once yours has moved, download statements from Capital One.
