Regional bank statement layouts: Regions, Truist, Huntington, Citizens and Fifth Third

Regions, Truist, Huntington, Citizens and Fifth Third each split a month differently. Some sort activity by kind, others put the direction in the headings or use two amount columns. This dataset lists what each bank typically prints and what breaks when the PDF becomes rows, with the arithmetic that catches each problem. ZeraBooks runs the same checks and proves every converted statement against its printed balances.

Files: layouts.csv (21 rows: bank, statement part, how it typically prints, what breaks, the check) and sample-months.csv (five fictional months, one per bank, with the section each row was printed in).

What do the sample months look like?

Each sample is a fictional business made for this dataset, shown as the converted rows. Every one ties: the beginning balance plus every signed row equals the ending balance the statement would print. All rows are in sample-months.csv.

Regions: Mobile Bay Tile Co., October 2026

Nine rows spread over five sections. Section totals: Deposits & Credits 7,418.90, Withdrawals 2,233.48, Fees 12.00, Automatic Transfers 1,854.62, Checks 3,280.00. The proof: 9,872.40 + 7,418.90 − 2,233.48 − 12.00 − 1,854.62 − 3,280.00 = 9,911.20. The 1,104.62 loan transfer is part principal, part interest; the loan statement splits it. Truist: Piedmont Pet Grooming, September 2026

One table, with a running balance on every line as in the Truist copy we have seen: 7,066.19, 6,453.72, 6,003.72, 8,979.07, 8,947.57, 5,559.45. Credits 4,815.35, debits 4,482.09. The proof: 5,226.19 + 4,815.35 − 4,482.09 = 5,559.45. The 31.50 Service Charges - Prior Period line is August's analysis charge, taken in September. Guide: Truist bank statement to Excel.

Huntington: Maumee River Cycles, September 2026

Two credits and three debits, with the fee on its own line. The proof, in the order of the summary: 3,941.07 + 3,645.45 − 1,913.89 − 15.00 = 5,657.63. Average Balance and Low Balance are left out of the file. Guide: Huntington bank statement to Excel.

Balance Calculation order: Previous Balance 11,508.26, Checks 1,615.00, Debits 2,214.74, Deposits & Credits 6,132.18, Interest Paid 1.37. Check 5021 for 1,560.00 is the only check, so checks and withdrawals/debits (3,688.01) stay separate in the summary. The proof: 7,615.92 + 5,567.80 − 1,560.00 − 3,688.01 = 7,935.71. How does ZeraBooks turn sections into one proven list?

ZeraBooks Convert reads each page as an image and takes the direction of each amount from the section or column it sits in, so money out gets a minus. Every row gets a Type (Deposit, Withdrawal, Fee, Transfer, Check) and its year from the statement period, and check numbers are kept. Summary lines, average balances, daily balance pages and analysis statements stay out of the rows. Then it adds the rows to the beginning balance and compares the result with the ending balance the bank printed, to the cent.

The other big-bank layouts are in the companion datasets: U.S. bank statement layouts (Citi, U.S. Bank, TD Bank, PNC, Amex) and bank statement formats (Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and more). The full method is on the ZeraBooks bank statement converter page.

FAQ

Why does a Regions statement list the same month in five places?

Regions typically sorts activity into Deposits & Credits, Withdrawals, Fees, Automatic Transfers and Checks, each with its own section and summary total. The amounts print without a sign. Merge the sections in date order with money out as a minus, and the beginning balance plus the rows equals the ending balance.

Should I convert the Truist Account Analysis statement?

No. It explains how the prior period's service charges were worked out. The charge itself is one line on the checking statement, described as Service Charges - Prior Period. Convert the checking statement; keep the analysis PDF if you want the fee broken down.

What do the (+) and (-) in Huntington headings mean?

They give the direction of every amount under that heading. Huntington's own sample statement prints Credits (+) and Debits (-) in the summary, with fees on their own Total Fees (-) line. The rows themselves print without a sign, so the sign has to come from the heading.

Is the Current Balance on a Citizens statement my balance today?

No. It is the balance at the end of the statement period. It should equal the Previous Balance on the next statement.

How far back can I download Fifth Third statements?

Fifth Third says 24 months of account history. Anything older has to be requested from Fifth Third, then converted like any other PDF.
