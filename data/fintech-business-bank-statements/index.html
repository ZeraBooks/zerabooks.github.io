Fintech business bank statements: Chime, Bluevine, Mercury, Relay, Novo and Brex

Chime, Bluevine, Mercury, Relay, Novo and Brex all issue monthly PDF statements, but each keeps them in its own menu and posts them on its own day. Is a fintech statement a real bank statement?

Yes. Chime and Bluevine both say they are financial technology companies, not banks, and name the bank that provides the accounts: The Bancorp Bank, N.A. or Stride Bank, N.A. for Chime, and Coastal Community Bank for Bluevine. The statement still carries a beginning and an ending balance with every transaction between them, which is what a bookkeeper or lender needs. For Mercury, Relay, Novo and Brex, our guides don't name the bank, so this dataset leaves it blank rather than guess.

Why download the PDF and not the CSV?

Five of the six offer a transaction export, CSV in every case, with OFX as well at Relay; we couldn't verify an export for Chime. An export lists rows for the dates you pick. It usually has no beginning or ending balance, so nothing tells you a row is missing. Bluevine's CSV is the exception with a Balance column, and it only reaches 18 months back. The PDF has both balances, so ZeraBooks can prove the month to the cent. More on the difference in the Bluevine statement to Excel guide.

FAQ

Which bank holds the money in a Chime account?

Chime says banking services are provided by The Bancorp Bank, N.A. or Stride Bank, N.A., Members FDIC. Chime itself is a financial technology company, not a bank.

Why does my Chime statement show $0 in one section?

Each section reports only its own account. Chime says one section can show $0 while money sits in another. Each account is proven on its own.

When is a Bluevine statement ready?

Bluevine makes statements available by the 5th of the following month. Until then, the CSV export covers the month up to yesterday, but there is no statement yet to prove it against.

Does Novo issue statements for part of a month?

No. Novo issues full monthly statements only, ready on the 1st of the next month, going back to when the account was opened.

Are Brex card and business account statements the same file?

No. They are separate statements under Statements and documents. A card statement is proven as a card, and a business account statement as a bank account.
