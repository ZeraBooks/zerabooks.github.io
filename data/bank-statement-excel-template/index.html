README

Bank statement Excel template

To turn a PDF bank statement into an Excel sheet you can trust, put one row per transaction under Date, Description, Money In and Money Out, then check that the printed opening balance plus money in minus money out equals the printed closing balance. This workbook does that check and shows the first row where the running balance stops matching.

How the Statement sheet works

Cell or column You type What it does
B1, B2 Account name and statement period Labels only
B3, B4 Opening and closing balance printed on the statement Inputs for the check
B5, C5 Nothing =ROUND(B3+SUM(E9:E508)-B4,2). 0.00 and "Ties to the cent" in green means every row is in. Anything else turns red and shows the gap.
B6, C6 Nothing Total money in and total money out, to compare with the statement's summary box
A to D, from row 9 Date, Description, Money In, Money Out Both amounts positive, as the bank prints them
E Amount Nothing Money In minus Money Out, so money out is negative
F Running balance Nothing Opening balance plus every Amount so far
G Printed balance The bank's running balance, where it prints one Optional
H Difference Nothing F minus G. The first row that turns red is where the error is.

How to use it

  1. Download bank-statement-template.xlsx and save a copy per statement, for example checking-2026-09.xlsx.
  2. Type the printed opening and closing balances in B3 and B4.
  3. Paste the transactions from row 9. If they came from a converter or a bank export with one signed amount column, put positives in Money In and negatives, without the minus, in Money Out.
  4. Add the bank's running balance in column G if the statement prints one.
  5. Read C5. If it says Ties to the cent, the sheet is ready to reconcile from or to import.

Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers and LibreOffice. Dates are formatted MM/DD/YYYY.

Reading a gap

Gap in B5 Usual cause
Exactly twice one amount That amount is in the wrong column, money in typed as money out
The amount of a real transaction That line is missing, often the first or last line on a page
A small amount that divides by 9 Two digits swapped on one row, such as 1,650.00 typed as 1,560.00

FAQ

Can Excel open a PDF bank statement directly?

Excel can pull some tables out of a PDF, but it doesn't split accounts, it struggles with descriptions that wrap onto a second line, and it never checks the totals. Whatever you use to get the rows out, this sheet is the check.

Why Money In and Money Out instead of one Amount column?

It is the layout most bookkeepers reconcile from, because it matches how the statement prints the figures. Column E gives you the single signed amount that accounting software imports want.

Why are some cells blank in my converted file?

When the statement doesn't print a value, such as the running balance on some lines, the cell should stay blank rather than be guessed. Column H skips rows with no printed balance.
