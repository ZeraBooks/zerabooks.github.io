About ZeraBooks

ZeraBooks is AI bookkeeping software with a general ledger and a bank statement converter that checks every statement against its printed opening and closing balance, and a done-for-you bookkeeping service run from Surrey, BC for clients in the U.S., Canada and elsewhere.
